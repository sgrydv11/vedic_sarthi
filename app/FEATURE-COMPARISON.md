# Sri Mandir and Avantika feature comparison

This comparison uses Sri Mandir's public website and linked product pages. It covers visible features and published descriptions. It does not assert how Sri Mandir implements its private services or what happens after purchase.

| Feature | Public Sri Mandir product | Avantika after this update | Remaining scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pooja | Dated temple poojas, account booking history and fulfillment evidence | Remote and in-person requests, Sankalp, role-based status, ritual video link and Prasad tracking | Live partner capacity, scheduled campaigns and payment settlement |
| Chadhava | Dated temple offerings, booking history and fulfillment evidence | Temple and offering selection, Sankalp, request tracking and staff-recorded fulfillment evidence | Partner scheduling and paid checkout |
| Temples and darshan | Temple directory and app darshan | Regional temple directory and operations-published HTTPS darshan entries | Larger directory and licensed recordings or streams |
| Personal mandir | Personal temple and virtual worship | Deity selection and private, date-based offering history across signed-in devices | Additional virtual rituals, media and reminders |
| Literature and music | Devotional articles, books, music, Aarti, Mantra and Chalisa | Festival and home worship guides, three original starter readings, Jaap Mala and published devotional audio links | Licensed media catalog, playlists and richer content |
| Panchang, horoscope, astrology | Daily material, online calculators and astrologer consultations | Published dated notes, horoscope and observance entries, existing review requests and staff-scheduled consultation links | Verified calculations, daily data feed and native live consultation |
| Community | Devotional community advertised in the app | Signed-in reflections, owner visibility while pending, and operations moderation before publication | Replies, following and notifications |
| Store | Product catalog and retail checkout | Product browsing and trackable quote requests | Inventory, payment collection, fulfillment and returns |

Sources: [Sri Mandir home and app overview](https://www.srimandir.com/) · [Chadhava](https://www.srimandir.com/chadhava) · [Temple directory](https://www.srimandir.com/temples) · [Astrology calculators](https://www.srimandir.com/astrology-calculator) · [Store](https://srimandir.store/).
